## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: a finished macOS application icon, square 1024x1024, for a native wallpaper app named Sceneveil / 幕景, which browses wallpaper images, loops videos and plays Steam Workshop animated scenes. Create ONE icon only, not a presentation board or mockup. The icon is a large centered macOS rounded-square tile on a truly transparent outer canvas, tile occupies about 90% of the image. Elegant native Apple-style dimensional icon with restrained satin-glass depth: a midnight-indigo rounded-square surface containing three flowing layered landscape silhouettes, a lavender distant ridge, a cobalt middle ridge and a vivid turquoise foreground veil sweeping upward softly. A small warm ivory sun sits above the horizon. The layers subtly form a folded curtain / open scene, evocative of a living desktop. Clean bold silhouette, balanced graphic composition, distinct and legible at 32px, generous breathing room, softly luminous edges, very subtle material shading, sophisticated and quiet. Keep details sparse, no star field, no tiny decoration. No letters, Chinese characters, words, badge, play triangle, monitor bezel, external drop shadow, watermark, brand logos or extra objects. The icon itself must be fully opaque within its rounded-square shape, with clean antialiased edges and real transparent alpha outside the tile.
